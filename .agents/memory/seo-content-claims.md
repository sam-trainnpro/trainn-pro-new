---
name: Public SEO content claims
description: Evidence requirements for public provider case studies and SEO scope.
---

Do not invent provider testimonials, earnings, satisfaction or retention metrics to fill a marketing resource. A provider success-story teaser should remain explicitly unfinished and non-indexable until attributable examples are available.

**Why:** The SEO audit found an unfinished case-study page presenting unsupported outcome statistics as results. No documented provider evidence was supplied to replace those claims.

**How to apply:** When publishing provider stories, require attributable examples and a clear basis for outcome metrics before presenting the page as a completed resource or making it indexable. Keep SEO remediation focused on content accuracy rather than new product flows.
